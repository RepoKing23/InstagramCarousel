## Posting Strategy Quick Reference

**Cadence:** 2–3 carousels per week is the sweet spot. Rotate the pillars: education (ideas 1–5), trust/authority (6, 8, 10), personality (9).

**CTA patterns that convert in this niche:**
- "DM me 'WORD'" (trackable, low-friction — best for consults)
- "Save this for your consult" (feeds the algorithm's favorite signal)
- "Send this to a friend who…" (shares = reach)
- Always end the final slide with a CTA + your booking path.

**Formatting rules:**
- Cover slide = one bold hook, max 8 words, in your yellow display type. The cover's only job is to earn the first swipe.
- One idea per slide. If a slide needs two sentences of body text, split it.
- Keep the LB monogram + "NP CLEO" badge consistent on every cover — it's your recognizability engine.

**Repurposing each carousel:**
- Read the slides aloud to camera → Reel
- Cover hook + poll → Story ("Which myth did you believe?")
- Q&A comments from idea #9 → next month's content topics

**Compliance reminder:** For any before/after or client-story content, use written consent, avoid outcome guarantees ("results may vary"), and follow your state's NP advertising rules.
