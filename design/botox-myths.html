The Lie 01
“Botox will freeze my face.”
The Truth
Correct dosing softens movement. It doesn’t erase it. You’ll still smile, laugh, and look like you. Frozen faces come from heavy hands, not from Botox itself.
@luxury_beauty_aestheticz
The Lie 02
“If I stop, my face will look worse.”
The Truth
You simply return to your baseline. Nothing snaps back, nothing ages faster. You just lose the smoothing. That’s all.
@luxury_beauty_aestheticz
The Lie 03
“It’s too early
for me.”
The Truth
Lines are easier to prevent than to erase. Preventative treatment in your late 20s and 30s is why some people never seem to age.

The Lie 04
“Botox always looks fake.”
The Truth
Great work is invisible. You’ve seen it a hundred times without knowing. Bad work is what gave Botox its reputation.
