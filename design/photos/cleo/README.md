# Client Result Photos

Converted from the original HEIC uploads to JPG (sRGB, quality 92, EXIF
rotation applied). Originals were removed after conversion. All 20 uploads are
here; nothing was dropped.

These are real client before and after photos, all lip filler. Before using any
of them in a post:

1. Confirm written consent from the client for social media use
2. **Crop every photo to the treatment area.** House rule as of Aug 2026: no
   eyes, no brows, no tattoos, no jewellery that identifies a client. Consent is
   on file, and the agreement is that faces stay covered
3. Never pair with outcome guarantees. Add "individual results vary"
4. Instagram and TikTok restrict paid promotion of before and after
   cosmetic imagery. Organic posts are fine, boosting them is not
